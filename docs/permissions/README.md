# Neno — content permission requests

Neno is a free, Swahili-first Seventh-day Adventist Bible and learning app for iOS and Android. It works offline, puts Swahili first, and also offers English and French. It is for SDA members, youth, elders and seekers in Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, DRC, Rwanda and Burundi.

These are draft letters. Before sending, replace the sender placeholders: `[SENDER NAME]`, `[ORGANISATION — e.g. SOCK LLC]`, `[EMAIL]`, `[PHONE]`, `[UNION/CONFERENCE ENDORSEMENT if any]`. **Do not ship any of this content in a public build until written permission is on file**, except the interim Bible text, which has an open licence.

## What each permission must cover

Do not treat a reply as cleared unless it covers, in writing:

1. **Scope of use**: in-app display on iOS and Android, in the named languages and editions.
2. **Offline storage**: caching or bundling on the device, including the format (e.g. encrypted SQLite packs) and any limits such as one quarter at a time or a book count.
3. **Modification**: whether reformatting, pagination, search indexing, excerpting (daily quotes) and cross-linking are allowed. Text stays verbatim in all cases.
4. **Audio** (where relevant): a separate grant for recordings, with performer and recording rights noted.
5. **Attribution**: the exact credit line and where it must appear (the content screen and/or the About screen).
6. **Term and territory**: worldwide or a named region, how long, and renewal.
7. **Fees**: confirm there are none, or state the terms. Neno is free with no ads.
8. **Revocation and takedown**: the notice period and how quickly we must remove content.
9. **Signatory**: the name and role of the person authorised to grant it, plus any API key or agreement reference.

Our standing commitments, repeated in every letter: free app, no ads, non-commercial; doctrinal review before anything is published; attribution shown in the app; content removed promptly on request; a demo build available.
