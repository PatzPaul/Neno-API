**Subject:** Request for a digital text licence for the Swahili Union Version (and Biblia Habari Njema) in the "Neno" app

Ndugu wapendwa, salamu katika jina la Bwana wetu Yesu Kristo.

We are building **Neno**, a free, Swahili-first Seventh-day Adventist Bible and learning app for iOS and Android. It serves members, youth, elders and seekers in Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, DRC, Rwanda and Burundi, and it works offline because many readers have limited or expensive data.

For most Swahili-speaking Adventists, the **Swahili Union Version (SUV, Biblia Takatifu)** is the Bible they read in church and memorise. Our members strongly prefer it. Until we have a licence, Neno uses Biblica's open **Neno: Biblia Takatifu (Kiswahili Contemporary Version)**, under CC BY-SA 4.0. We would like to offer the SUV as the main text.

**We respectfully ask for:**

- A **digital text licence for the SUV** for in-app display on iOS and Android. We would also welcome **Biblia Habari Njema** if available.
- Delivery of the text as **USX/USFM through the Digital Bible Library**. We are ready to apply as a DBL publishing partner (LCH) if you prefer that route.
- Permission for **offline storage** of the full text on the device, in an app-internal format.
- Permission for **parallel display** with other licensed or open translations (for example SUV beside an English or French Bible), plus verse sharing of short passages with attribution.
- The **copyright and attribution line** you require, and any reporting you need (for example annual usage figures).

**Our commitments:**

- Free app, no ads, non-commercial.
- Text displayed without alteration.
- A doctrinal review step before publication.
- Attribution shown with the text and in the About screen.
- Prompt takedown or updates on request.

We would be glad to share a **demo build** and to work with both Societies together, whichever is the right licensing party for each edition.
