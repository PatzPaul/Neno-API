**Title:** Permission request: showing Sabbath School lessons (sw/en/fr) from your API in Neno, a Swahili-first SDA app

Hello Adventech team, and thank you for the Sabbath School app and API — it is a blessing to the church.

We are building **Neno**, a free Seventh-day Adventist Bible and learning app for iOS and Android, Swahili first with
English and French, for members, youth and seekers in Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, DRC, Rwanda and Burundi. It has no ads
and no paid features, and works offline for people on limited data.

We would like to show the **Adult Sabbath School Bible Study Guide** in Swahili, English and French using your public
API (`sabbath-school.adventech.io/api/v2`). We understand the content is © General Conference, all rights reserved,
so we are asking before we use it:

- **Display** the current quarterly — lesson, daily readings, memory text, questions — exactly as published, unmodified.
- **Cache the current quarter on the device** so it can be read offline, refreshed from your API.
- **Attribution** on every lesson screen, in the wording you prefer (e.g. "© General Conference of Seventh-day
  Adventists · via the Sabbath School app by Adventech"), with a link to your app.
- **Respectful API use**: server-side caching, rate limits, a User-Agent identifying Neno, and any API key or terms you
  require.
- **Takedown**: we remove the content promptly if you or the GC ask.

Two questions:

1. Can Adventech grant this, or should we write to the GC Sabbath School & Personal Ministries Department (or the
   East-Central Africa Division) — and is there a contact you would recommend?
2. Are audio and cover images covered by the same permission, or separately?

We are happy to share a demo build, and to send Swahili corrections back through your contribution process.
