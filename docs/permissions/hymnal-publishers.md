# Hymnal permission requests

Two letters follow: (A) Nyimbo za Kristo and (B) the Seventh-day Adventist Hymnal (1985). Each asks for **text** and **audio** as separate grants.

Background for both: many hymns are public domain (older texts and tunes), but others have copyrights per hymn (for words, music or translation), often held by third parties. Wherever possible, we ask the publisher for a rights list.

---

**Endorsement:** [UNION/CONFERENCE ENDORSEMENT if any]

**Subject:** Ombi la ruhusa / Request for permission to include *Nyimbo za Kristo* in the "Neno" app

Wapendwa katika Kristo, Bwana Yesu asifiwe.

We are building **Neno**, a free, Swahili-first Seventh-day Adventist Bible and learning app for iOS and Android. It is for members, youth, elders and seekers across East and Central Africa, and it works offline. Members often ask to carry *Nyimbo za Kristo* on their phones for Sabbath worship, choir practice and family worship.

**We respectfully ask for:**

1. **Text:** permission to display the lyrics of *Nyimbo za Kristo* (with hymn numbers and titles) in the app, and to store the text offline.
2. **Audio (separate request):** permission to include or stream recordings (choir and/or piano accompaniment) that you own or can license, or guidance on who holds those rights.
3. **Rights guidance:** a list, or general guidance, of which hymns are public domain or Church-owned and which are held by third parties (for example translated hymns with copyrighted originals).
4. The **attribution wording** you require.

**Our commitments:** free app, no ads, non-commercial; lyrics shown verbatim, with numbering matching the printed hymnal; doctrinal review before publication; attribution shown in-app; prompt takedown on request. We would be happy to send a **demo build**.

Asanteni sana na Mungu awabariki.
