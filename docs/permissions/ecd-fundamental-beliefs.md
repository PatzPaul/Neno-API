Seekers and baptismal classes often ask, "What do Adventists believe?" We would like Neno to answer with the Church's own words: the **28 Fundamental Beliefs**, in the **official Swahili translation**, alongside English and French. Each belief would link to the Bible texts it cites.

**We respectfully ask for:**

- Permission to **display the full text** of the 28 Fundamental Beliefs, verbatim, in Swahili (official translation), English and French.
- Permission to **store the text offline** in the app.
- Permission to make the **cited Scripture references tappable**, so they open the verse in the app's Bible. The belief text itself would not change.
- The **authoritative source file** for the Swahili (and French, if held by ECD) translation, or a pointer to it, so we use the current approved wording.
- The **attribution wording** you require (for example "© General Conference of Seventh-day Adventists").
- Confirmation of **who holds the rights**: whether ECD can grant this for the Swahili text, or whether we should go to the General Conference.

- Free app, no ads, non-commercial.
- Verbatim text, with no paraphrase or commentary presented as official.
- A doctrinal review step before publication, with input from local church leadership.
- Attribution shown with the beliefs and in the About screen.
- Updates applied promptly if the wording is revised, and takedown on request.
